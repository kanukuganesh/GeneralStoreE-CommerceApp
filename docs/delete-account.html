How to delete your account — GS (Greeno) Grocery App

App package: com.kanukuganesh.generalstore · Developer: Kanuku Ganesh · Last updated 17 October 2026

You can delete your GS grocery account at any time, directly inside the app. Deletion is permanent and removes your personal data from our systems.

Delete your account in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the GS grocery app and sign in with your username and password.
  2. Go to the Account tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is deleted immediately.

What is deleted

  • Profile (name, username, phone number)
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Order history visible in the app
  • Wishlist and cart data

What may be kept

  • Payment transaction records (transaction ID, amount, status) may be retained by our payment provider Razorpay and in payment-related order records where required by Indian law for accounting and fraud prevention. These records are not used to contact you.
  • Technical logs may be retained briefly for security, then deleted.

No account access? Other ways to request deletion

  • If you cannot sign in, open an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/kanukuganesh/GeneralStoreE-CommerceApp/issues — include your username and registered phone number, and we will delete your account and confirm by reply.
