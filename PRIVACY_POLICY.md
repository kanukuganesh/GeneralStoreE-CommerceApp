# Privacy Policy — GS (Greeno) Grocery App

**Last updated:** 14 October 2026
**App name:** GS (Greeno)
**Package / app ID:** `com.kanukuganesh.generalstore`
**Developer:** Kanuku Ganesh

This Privacy Policy explains what information the GS grocery app ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the App you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Overview

GS is an online grocery shopping app. To work, it needs certain information from you — for example your name and delivery address so we can deliver orders, and your location (only if you allow it) so we can autofill your address and show your delivery point on a map.

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do **not** show third‑party advertising. We do **not** use advertising or marketing trackers.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
| Data | Why we need it |
|------|----------------|
| **Name** | To address you correctly and deliver orders |
| **Username** | To identify your account and look up your orders |
| **Phone number** | So the delivery rider / store can contact you about your order |
| **Password** | To secure your account (stored only as a secure hash — we never see or store your plain password) |
| **Delivery addresses** (house, area/colony, city, PIN code, mandal, district, state) | To deliver your order to the right place |
| **Order contents & history** | To process, fulfil and track your orders |
| **Wishlist / cart** | To save products you want and complete purchases |

### 2.2 Location (only with your permission)
- The App asks for your **device location permission** (precise / approximate) **only when you tap** "Use my GPS location" or interact with the map address picker.
- Your coordinates (latitude/longitude) are used to **autofill your delivery address** and to **show your location on the map** for delivery.
- **Location is never collected in the background** and never when you are not actively using the address feature. You can decline the permission and still add an address manually.

### 2.3 Information collected automatically
- **Cart, session and preferences** are stored locally on your device (using browser local storage) so the App remembers your login and basket.
- We do **not** collect device advertising IDs, contact lists, photos, files, SMS, or call logs.

### 2.4 Payments
- Payments are made through **UPI apps** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) installed on your device, or by **Cash on Delivery**.
- The App **does not collect, store or process** your card number, bank account, UPI PIN or any payment credentials. Payment happens entirely inside your chosen UPI app / in cash.

---

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and secure your account.
- To process, fulfil, deliver and track your orders.
- To autofill addresses using your location (only when you ask).
- To contact you about an order (delivery, availability, changes).
- To show your order history and status (Placed → Packed → Out for delivery → Delivered).
- To keep the service secure and prevent fraud/abuse.

---

## 4. Where your data is stored

- Account, addresses, orders and wishlist are stored in our **cloud database (Supabase)** hosted on secure, encrypted servers.
- Cart, login session and some preferences are stored **locally on your device**.
- Data is transmitted over **encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)**.

---

## 5. Who we share data with

We share **only the minimum necessary** with service providers that help run the App, and never sell your data:

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Supabase** | Cloud database & login | Account, addresses, orders (stored securely) |
| **OpenStreetMap / Nominatim** | Reverse‑geocoding your GPS coordinates into a readable address | Coordinates only (sent when you use the address/map feature) |
| **OpenStreetMap tiles (Leaflet)** | Displaying the address map | Map requests (standard map tile usage) |

Your delivery address and phone may be visible to the **store/delivery staff** fulfilling your order, for the sole purpose of delivering it.

We may disclose information if **required by law** or to protect the rights, safety and property of the App, its users, or the public.

---

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Access / correct** your profile and addresses anytime in **Account**.
- **Delete your account** in **Account → Delete account**. This removes your profile, orders and addresses from our systems.
- **Location permission** can be denied or revoked anytime in your device settings; the App still works with manual addresses.
- **Opt out** of nothing marketing‑wise — we send no marketing emails or ads.

---

## 7. Children

The App is intended for adults who can enter into a purchase contract. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 8. Data security

- Passwords are stored only as **one‑way cryptographic hashes** — they cannot be read or reversed.
- All cloud traffic uses **TLS encryption**.
- Access to data is protected by **row‑level security** rules: you can only see your own data; only the store admin can see orders to fulfil them.
- Payment credentials are **never** handled or stored by the App.
- If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we follow industry best practices to protect your information.

## 11. Location permission disclosure (Play Store requirement)

The App requests **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION** and **ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION**. These are used **only** to autofill and pin your delivery address while you actively use the address / map feature. They are **not** used to track you in the background, and are not used for advertising.
